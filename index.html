M.D. Sky OS

Logistics operations platform

Live
mdskyos.com (opens in new tab) ↗
Role
Design, build, configuration, testing and deployment
Stack
Supabase · PostgreSQL · Google sign-in · Cloudflare · GitHub

A trucking company’s owner, dispatchers, drivers and customers each work in their own interface, on shared data. Who can see and change what is enforced at the data layer — not just hidden in the menus.

  1. Customer requests a shipment
  2. Dispatch reviews and assigns
  3. Driver accepts and moves the load
  4. Status is recorded at each step
  5. Owner & customer see where it stands
  1. 01

    Four interfaces, one dataset. Separate experiences for owners, dispatchers, drivers and customers, so each sees only their part of the work.

  2. 02

    Sign-in, boundaries, integrations. Google authentication, role rules on the database, custom domain and DNS on Cloudflare, and third-party service integration.

  3. 03

    Tested from every side. Each change is checked as every affected role — including attempts it should refuse — and tracked in Git so it can be rolled back.

Testing practice & project status
  • Browser-based QA through each role’s workflow, end to end.
  • Permission and database-security checks: confirming access is refused where it should be, not only granted where it should be.
  • Bugs fixed, then the affected workflows re-tested.
  • Implementation, testing and release coordinated directly with the business owner.

Live: role-based app, authentication, core workflows, hosting and domain. In progress: additional third-party integrations, including transportation-management, and full rollout with the client’s team.

A pet-care business needs more than a date from each booking: the service, the schedule and the pickup and drop-off arrangements. The booking flow collects exactly that, and each request lands in the admin portal as one complete record.

How I work through a problem

Establish who is affected, follow the request through sign-in, access, the application and its data, then test a single explanation before acting on it. Each change is deliberate, recorded and reversible — and nothing is closed until the person who reported it confirms it works.
