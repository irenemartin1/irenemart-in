How I approach a problem

  1. People first. Restore service. Identify who is blocked and the impact on their work.
  2. Understand the whole system. Consider the account, permissions, device, network/DNS, application, and data.
  3. Reproduce and isolate. Capture the exact error, timing, and recent changes; test one hypothesis at a time.
  4. Fix carefully. Restore service when possible, apply the appropriate fix, and be ready to roll back.
  5. Close the loop. Verify with the user, document the cause and solution, and escalate when an issue exceeds my access or expertise.
