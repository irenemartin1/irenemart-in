Operating principles

  1. User impact firstScope by who is blocked and what they can’t do.

  3. Least privilegeAccess matches role. Verified, not assumed.
  4. Close the loopConfirm with the user. Document cause and fix.
  5. Leave it documentedNo undocumented settings. The next admin starts informed.
