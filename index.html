How I troubleshoot

  1. Find who’s blocked. Restore service first. Know who is affected and how badly.
  2. Check the whole chain. Account, permissions, device, network/DNS, application, data.
  3. Reproduce it. Exact error, time, recent changes. Test one cause at a time.
  4. Fix it safely. Apply the fix within policy, with a rollback ready.
  5. Close it out. Confirm with the user, document cause and fix, escalate what’s beyond my access.
