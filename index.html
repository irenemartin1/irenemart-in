How I troubleshoot

I treat every issue as a system problem, not a single symptom. I define who is affected, decide what matters most, check every layer, find the root cause, and resolve it so it doesn’t come back.

  1. Define the scope and users Find out who is blocked, how many people, which roles or locations, since when, and what work has stopped. One user and a whole department are different problems.
  2. Prioritize by impact Widespread or work-stopping issues come first. If people can’t work, I restore service with a safe workaround or rollback, then investigate.
  3. Check the whole system I go layer by layer: account and permissions, device, network and DNS, application, data and integrations. Compare a user who works with one who doesn’t to narrow it down.
  4. Find the root cause I reproduce it, capture the exact error, check recent changes, and test one cause at a time. A reset that clears the symptom isn’t the answer until I know why it happened.
  5. Resolve, verify, close the loop I apply the resolution within policy with a rollback ready, confirm it with the affected users, document the cause and resolution, update the guide or setting that prevents a repeat, and escalate anything beyond my access.
