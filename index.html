How I work

Straightforward, in phases, with a working system at the end of each one.

  1. Understand the business We talk through how you sell, serve and operate today, and where time and money leak.
  2. Demo before you commit I build a clickable demo around your business so you see it before paying for it.
  3. Build in phases Phase one goes live in weeks, not months. Every phase is tested by role before launch.
  4. Launch & train I set up accounts and permissions, move your data over and train your team in English or Spanish.
  5. Support & grow Hosting, fixes and improvements on a monthly plan, adding new pieces as the business grows.
